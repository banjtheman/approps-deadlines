U.S. Senate Committee on Appropriations 
Subcommittee on Homeland Security 
Outside Witness Testimony Instructions 
Fiscal Year 2027 Appropriations 
The Senate Subcommittee on Homeland Security has not scheduled any outside witness 
hearings on appropriations for fiscal year 2027.  However, as in past years, written 
testimony will be accepted in accordance with the guidelines below. 
GUIDELINES 
Format: 
• Testim
ony (including any supporting material) should be a maximum of four (4)
pages, on 8 ½” x 11” paper, single sided, single spaced, and have a 1” margin.
• Do not include a cover page.
• At the top of the first page, list the name of the person or organization submitting
testimony; that it is prepared for the Subcommittee on Homeland Security; and
which Department and/or Agency the testimony is addressing.
Delivery: 
• Delivery is by electronic submission only, as described below.  Other forms of 
delivery, including fax and delivery through the mail, will not be accepted.
• Please e-mail testimony to homelandsec@appro.senate.gov with the subject line 
“FY27 Homeland OWT”.
• To facilitate printing, we can only accept testimony in Microsoft Word or Word 
Perfect formats.  DO NOT SEND PDF FILES.
• In the email, please include contact information (name, email, physical address 
and telephone).
Deadline: 
• All material must be received no later than close of business, Sunday, May 
24, 2026.  This deadline will be strictly enforced.
Thank you.
