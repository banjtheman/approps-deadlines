U.S. Senate Committee on Appropriations 
Subcommittee on Military Construction, Veterans Affairs, and Related Agencies 
Outside Witness Testimony Instructions 
Fiscal Year 2027 Appropriations 
The Senate Subcommittee on Military Construction, Veterans Affairs, and Related 
Agencies has not scheduled any outside witness hearings on appropriations for fiscal year 
2027.  However, as in past years, written testimony will be accepted in accordance with 
the guidelines below. 
GUIDELINES 
Policy: 
• For outside witne
ss testimony to be considered by the Subcommittee, it must be at
the request of a personal office of a Member of the U.S. Senate Committee on
Appropriations.  Testimony sent directly to the Subcommittee will not be
considered.
Format: 
• Testi
mony (including any supporting material) should be a maximum of four (4)
pages, on 8 ½” x 11” paper, single sided, single spaced, and have a 1” margin.
• Do not include a cover page.
• At the top of the first page, list the name of the person or organization submitting
testimony.
Delivery: 
• Delivery
 is by electronic submission only, as described above.  Other forms of
delivery, including fax and delivery through the mail, will not be accepted.
• To facilitate printing, we can only accept testimony in Microsoft Word or Word
Perfect formats.  DO NOT SEND PDF FILES.
• In the submission, please include contact information (name, email, physical
address and telephone).
Deadline:
 
• All material must be received no later than close of business, Friday, May 15,
2026.  This deadline will be strictly enforced.
Thank you.
